## Spacing

A 4px grid: the step number times 4 is the size in pixels, so `--uoa-space-4` is 16px.

<SpacingScale />

## Containers

<TokenTable prefix="container-" />

`.uoa-container` centres content at `--uoa-container-max` with side gutters;
add `.uoa-container--narrow` for long-form text.

## Layout helpers

Compose these instead of writing one-off margins. Each takes a custom property to change its gap.

<Example
  title=".uoa-stack — vertical rhythm (--uoa-stack-gap)"
  code={`
    <div class="uoa-stack" style="--uoa-stack-gap: var(--uoa-space-3)">
      <div style="padding: 1rem; background: #f1f5f9; border-radius: 8px">Πρώτο</div>
      <div style="padding: 1rem; background: #f1f5f9; border-radius: 8px">Δεύτερο</div>
      <div style="padding: 1rem; background: #f1f5f9; border-radius: 8px">Τρίτο</div>
    </div>
  `}
/>

<Example
  title=".uoa-cluster — inline group that wraps (--uoa-cluster-gap)"
  code={`
    <div class="uoa-cluster">
      <button class="uoa-button" type="button">Υποβολή</button>
      <button class="uoa-button uoa-button--secondary" type="button">Αποθήκευση</button>
      <a href="#">Βοήθεια</a>
    </div>
  `}
/>

<Example
  title=".uoa-grid — as many columns as fit (--uoa-grid-min, --uoa-grid-gap)"
  code={`
    <div class="uoa-grid" style="--uoa-grid-min: 10rem">
      <div style="padding: 1.5rem; background: #f1f5f9; border-radius: 8px">1</div>
      <div style="padding: 1.5rem; background: #f1f5f9; border-radius: 8px">2</div>
      <div style="padding: 1.5rem; background: #f1f5f9; border-radius: 8px">3</div>
      <div style="padding: 1.5rem; background: #f1f5f9; border-radius: 8px">4</div>
    </div>
  `}
/>
